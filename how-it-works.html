Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SwiftShield™ dual escrow vault protect my money?
When you book a service, your payment is debited and transferred into a secure, regulated escrow holding vault. The artisan is alerted that the job is fully funded and begins work immediately. The artisan does not receive the funds until you physically inspect the work, verify it functions properly, and click "Release Funds" from your dashboard. If a job is abandoned or substandard, our dispute desk arbitrates or refunds your deposit.
What criteria must an artisan meet to become verified on SwiftErrand?
Every artisan undergoes a rigorous 4-tier accreditation check: (1) Official identity verification using the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) database via NIN; (2) Physical workshop and residential address inspection by our Uyo field team; (3) Peer reference check from recognized trade guilds (e.g. Electrical, Plumbing, NATA); and (4) Practical test job evaluation.
How are prices determined? Can an artisan change the price on arrival?
Standard jobs (such as diagnostic scans, AC gas refills, or routine servicing) carry transparent platform benchmark rates. For custom jobs, the artisan provides an itemized labor quote before commencing work. Once you agree and fund the escrow, the price is locked. An artisan cannot unilaterally raise prices on-site. If unforeseen replacement parts are needed, an official variation request must be digitally logged and approved by you.
How quickly can an artisan arrive at my location in Uyo?
Our automated dispatch algorithm matches you with active, verified technicians stationed within your immediate quadrant. In central zones such as Shelter Afrique, Ewet Housing, Osongama, and Oron Road, average technician arrival time is 15 to 25 minutes. You can monitor their live GPS approach on your interactive tracking screen.
I am an artisan. How quickly do I receive my money after finishing a job?
Instantly. The moment the customer marks the job complete and signs off, the escrow funds are immediately deposited into your SwiftWallet. From there, you can click "Withdraw" and transfer funds directly into your Nigerian bank account (Access, GTB, Zenith, OPay, PalmPay, Kuda) within seconds with zero delay.
